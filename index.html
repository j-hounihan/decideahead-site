01

Scenario training works.
Building it doesn't.

Everyone agrees rehearsing decisions under pressure makes better commanders. The trouble has always been standing up the training.

SLOW

Weeks to author

A realistic scenario takes hours — or a vendor content team. The training calendar has moved on by the time it's ready.

COSTLY

Six figures, one room

Live drills burn a shift. Legacy simulators run into the hundreds of thousands, with hardware, in a single room.

UNPROVEN

Can't show readiness

So teams recycle the same tired drills — and can't demonstrate their people are actually ready when it counts.
