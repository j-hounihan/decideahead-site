The problem

Scenario training works. Building it doesn't.

Everyone agrees rehearsing decisions under pressure makes better commanders. The trouble is standing up the training.

1

Slow

A realistic scenario takes hours — or a vendor content team — to author. The training calendar has moved on by the time it's ready.

2

Costly

Live drills burn a shift. Legacy simulators run into six figures with hardware and live in a single room.

3

Unproven

So teams recycle the same tired drills — and can't show their people are actually ready when it counts.
